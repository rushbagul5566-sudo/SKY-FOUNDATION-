About Sky Foundation

Our commitment to positive change

Our Mission

To empower communities through accessible healthcare, organizational development, and cooperative initiatives that foster sustainable positive change.

Our Vision

A world where every individual has access to quality healthcare, education, and opportunities to grow within a cooperative, inclusive society.

Our Objectives

  • Improve healthcare accessibility
  • Build strong communities
  • Promote education & skills
  • Foster cooperation & unity

Why Choose Us

  • Community-driven: Local solutions
  • Dedicated: Passionate team

Our Services

We provide comprehensive social services to empower communities

Health Camps

Regular health check-up camps in rural areas providing free medical consultation and basic treatment.

Home Healthcare Support

Dedicated healthcare professionals visit homes to provide medical care for elderly and bedridden patients.

Elderly Care

Comprehensive support including nutrition, medical aid, and companionship for senior citizens.

Women Empowerment

Skill development, financial literacy, and awareness programs to strengthen women independence.

Child Welfare

Educational support, nutrition programs, and safe shelter for underprivileged children.

Education Support

Scholarship programs, tuition classes, and learning resources for economically disadvantaged students.

Blood Donation

Organized blood donation camps connecting donors with patients in critical need.

Disaster Relief

Emergency response and rehabilitation support during natural calamities and crises.

Premium Services

Specialized consulting and dedicated support for community development projects.
