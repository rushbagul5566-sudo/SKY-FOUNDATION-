Who We Are

Sky Foundation is a non-profit healthcare and social impact organization dedicated to bridging the gap between medical necessities and vulnerable communities. We blend professional care with deep empathy.

Our Vision

To create a society where quality healthcare, dignifying elderly support, and essential medical resources are accessible to every individual, regardless of their socioeconomic status.

Our Mission

Delivering sustainable home healthcare support, organizing rural medical camps, providing emergency aid, and fostering a passionate volunteer network committed to human service.

Our Values

Empathy, Transparency, Quality Healthcare, Selfless Service, and Unwavering Commitment to dignity for senior citizens, patients, and marginalized families.

What We Do

Comprehensive Healthcare & Community Services

Home Healthcare Support

Professional bedside nursing, wound care, and personalized medical assistance right in the comfort of patients' homes.

Patient Care Services

Dedicated post-operative care, chronic disease monitoring, and daily living assistance for recovering patients.

Elderly Care

Compassionate physical and emotional care for senior citizens, ensuring safety, dignity, and proper health management.

Medical Assistance

Helping underprivileged patients access essential medicines, diagnostic tests, and specialized equipment.

Health Awareness Programs

Educational workshops on preventive care, hygiene, nutrition, maternal health, and lifestyle disease control.

Free Medical Camps

Regular health checkup drives in rural and underserved urban pockets, offering free doctor consultations and medications.

Doctor Consultation Support

Connecting patients with qualified specialist physicians for tele-consultation and second opinions.

Emergency Support

Rapid coordination during medical emergencies, helping families navigate urgent hospitalizations and blood supply.
