About Sky Foundation

A clear horizon starts with steady ground.

Sky Foundation began with a simple belief — that every child, family and neighbourhood deserves the conditions to look up and aim higher. We partner with local schools, clinics and self-help groups rather than working around them, so progress stays in the hands of the people who live it every day.

Today our work spans education support, mobile health camps, vocational training and neighbourhood greening — four threads of one goal: room to grow.
