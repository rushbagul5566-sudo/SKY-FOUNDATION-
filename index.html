About Sky Foundation

Learn about our journey, mission and the impact we create

Who We Are

Sky Foundation is a registered non-profit organization dedicated to serving humanity. We work across healthcare, education, women empowerment, environmental protection and disaster relief.

Our Mission

To provide quality healthcare, education, and emergency relief to every needy person with transparency and compassion.

Our Vision

Creating a compassionate Maharashtra where every family has access to healthcare, education and equal opportunities.

Our Values

Transparency, Humanity, Equality, Compassion and Social Responsibility guide every initiative of Sky Foundation.

Our Journey

2018

Founded

Sky Foundation was established with a vision to serve marginalized communities.

2019

First Medical Camp

Organized first health camp reaching 500+ villagers across Sambhajinagar district.

2021

Education Program Launch

Started education support program helping 200+ underprivileged children.

2023

Women Empowerment

Launched skill development program benefiting 300+ women across 15 districts.

What We Do

Our Services

Comprehensive programs designed to transform lives and communities

Health Camps

Free medical check-ups, diagnostics and medicines for rural communities.

Home Healthcare

Medical support and care for elderly and bedridden patients at home.

Education Support

Scholarships, study materials and tuition for underprivileged children.

Women Empowerment

Skill training, self-employment and financial literacy programs.

Child Welfare

Nutrition programs, healthcare and protection for underprivileged children.

Blood Donation

Organizing blood donation camps and maintaining blood bank services.

Disaster Relief

Emergency aid and rehabilitation during natural calamities.

Environment Care

Tree plantation, waste management and environmental awareness programs.
