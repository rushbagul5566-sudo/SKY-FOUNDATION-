About Sky Foundation

Sky Foundation is a non-profit organization committed to creating a healthier, safer and stronger society. We work in healthcare, education, blood donation, women empowerment, child welfare, senior citizen support, environmental protection and disaster relief.

Our Causes

🏥 Healthcare

Free medical camps, patient support and health awareness.

📚 Education

Helping children with education, books and scholarships.

🩸 Blood Donation

Organizing blood donation drives and emergency support.

👩 Women Empowerment

Skill development and self-employment opportunities.

👴 Senior Citizen Support

Care, assistance and wellbeing programs for senior citizens.

🌱 Environment

Tree plantation, cleanliness drives and environmental awareness.
