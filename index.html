About Sky Foundation

Serving Humanity with Care & Compassion

Sky Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to improving healthcare, education, environmental protection and social welfare across Maharashtra.

Our Mission

To provide quality healthcare, education and social support for every needy person while promoting humanity and equality.

Our Vision

Building a healthy, educated and empowered society where everyone has equal opportunities.

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Transparency, Respect, Teamwork and Social Responsibility.
