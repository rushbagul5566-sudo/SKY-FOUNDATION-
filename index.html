About Sky Foundation

Serving Humanity with Care & Compassion

Sky Foundation is a Maharashtra based NGO dedicated to healthcare, education, women empowerment, environmental protection and social welfare.

We believe every person deserves dignity, hope and equal opportunity.

  • Healthcare & Medical Support
  • Education for Children
  • Women Empowerment
  • Environmental Awareness
  • Emergency Relief Programs
Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Together we build a healthier, educated and empowered society.

Our Mission

To provide quality healthcare, education, emergency relief, women empowerment and social support to every needy person.

Our Vision

Creating a compassionate Maharashtra where every family has access to healthcare, education and opportunities.

Our Values

Transparency, Humanity, Equality, Compassion and Social Responsibility guide every initiative of Sky Foundation.
